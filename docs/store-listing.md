# SideStage store listing

English store listing draft, updated 17 November 2026. This is not a claim of store approval.

## Listing name

SideStage: Karaoke Queue and Second-Screen Player (English manifest name; the short name is SideStage)

## English short description

Run karaoke from your laptop. Queue YouTube videos and local songs, give everyone a turn, and put the party on the big screen.

## English overview

Find it. Queue it. Sing it.

Turn your laptop and a TV or second screen into karaoke night. SideStage puts you in charge of the lineup, with the controls beside you and the performance on the big screen. Pick the songs, call up the next singer, and keep the party moving.

🎶 Take requests. Give everyone a turn.

Found the perfect song on YouTube? Add it to the queue while the current singer keeps going. Mix YouTube videos with your own audio and video files, add singer names, and drag the lineup into order. When someone is ready for their moment, move their song to the top.

🎤 Your laptop is the booth. Your TV is the stage.

Keep the queue and playback controls in Chrome's side panel while the player runs in a separate window. Choose a connected display so everyone can watch the performance, with the next singer and song shown on screen. Pause, skip, adjust the volume, or cue up what comes next from your laptop.

🎵 Keep the mood between songs.

Give singers a moment to swap places while background music plays. Set a separate volume for breaks, add an image or your own on-screen party title, and choose when the next performance begins. Start it yourself when the room is ready, or set a delay to keep things rolling automatically.

🔁 Make room for an encore.

Bring a crowd favorite back from the sung list without searching again. If a song is already waiting in the queue, SideStage gives you a heads-up before adding another copy. From the first request to the last singalong, you can see who's next and keep the lineup moving.

No SideStage account or subscription is required. Your queue, singer names, settings, and imported files stay in your browser profile. Video title lookups and playback connect directly to YouTube.

Before the first song

Bring your own YouTube links or local media. SideStage doesn't supply songs, generate lyrics, or remove vocals. YouTube's player, advertising, sign-in requirements, and autoplay restrictions still apply.

Keep the side panel open during playback. Other YouTube tabs are muted while it is open and are not automatically unmuted afterward. Changing the player's display or reopening it can restart the song. Developed around macOS and a second display; other desktop setups have not been verified.
